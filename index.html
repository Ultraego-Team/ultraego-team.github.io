Tink Tink Boom: Dig Survivor

Dig. Shoot. Go boom.

Crack rock into bursts of ore, outlast the horde and drop deeper. One thumb, six caverns, eighteen bosses.

Free on iPhone. Optional in-app purchases.

The game

Every block cracks. Every run is new.

Drag to move. Your repeater fires on its own. Walk into rock and it cracks open. Clear the wave, dig open the shaft and jump down to the next floor. Then go deeper.

1

Dig

Walk into rock to dig it out. Every block cracks, chips and bursts into ore.

2

Shoot

Your repeater fires on its own. You steer, dodge and choose where to dig.

3

Survive

Monsters pour out of the dark. Carve your own path, or let them come to you.

4

Choose

Level up mid-run and pick a power from a fresh draw. Stack it, evolve it, break the game.
